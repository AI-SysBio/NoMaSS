# qPCR time courses after EGF stimulation

Fold change of mature (`mRNA_*`) and nascent (`nRNA_*`) RNA relative to the
level at stimulation, for four genes responding to epidermal growth factor
(EGF), digitised from the figures of

> Zeisel A., et al. Coupled pre-mRNA and mRNA dynamics unveil operational strategies underlying transcriptional responses to stimuli. *Molecular Systems Biology* 7, 529 (2011). doi:10.1038/msb.2011.62

Columns: `*_X` is the time after stimulation in minutes, `*_Y` the fold change.
Only the mature mRNA columns are used by the NoMaSS scripts (Fig. 4B, Fig. S7, Table S3).
